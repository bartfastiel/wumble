# Attribution

`entchen-commons.pgm` is a grayscale copy (805 × 348 px, binary PGM) of
[File:Alle meine Entchen.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alle_meine_Entchen.jpg) from Wikimedia Commons,
a rendering of [File:Alle meine Entchen.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alle_meine_Entchen.svg) by
Frank Murmann (changes by Rabanus Flavus), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Only the colour depth was changed. It serves as a
real-world test image for the sheet scanner.

`example-clean.pgm` and `example-photo.pgm` are the example sheet ("Alle meine Entchen", public domain) as the app
draws it – once clean, once as a simulated photo. `expected.json` holds what the scanner read from all three images.
