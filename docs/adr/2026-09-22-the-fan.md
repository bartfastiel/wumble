# The fan: the extremes turn away

## Context

Eight octaves side by side on one screen leave every stripe too narrow. But the outer octaves
are rarely used, so they do not need equal space — they only need to exist.

## Decision

The stripes rotate continuously. In the middle they stand upright and span the full height;
towards the left they lean further and move downwards into the bass; towards the right they
lean the other way and move up into the treble. They also get shorter and narrower outwards.

## Why

The idea came from the player: at the edges, go vertical instead of horizontal. Rotating rather
than switching layouts keeps a single continuous rule, so there is no seam to learn, and the
direction of travel is readable from the tilt alone.

The rotation changes smoothly from stripe to stripe, which keeps neighbours nearly parallel and
is what allows a large total rotation without any overlap.

## Rejected

_Two rows of stripes, bass below and treble above._ A seam in the middle, and the reading
direction jumps.

_Simply showing fewer octaves._ Was the state before, and the missing range was noticed.
