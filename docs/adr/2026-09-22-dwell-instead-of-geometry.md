# Dwelling chooses, sweeping passes through

Date: 2026-09-22 · Status: accepted

## Context

Subdominant and dominant follow each other constantly and have no tone in common. On the map
they are two steps apart, with the tonic between them, so dragging from one to the other
sounds the tonic on the way.

## Decision

A chord is taken only when the finger rests on it for about 95 ms. A quick sweep passes over
intermediate chords without sounding them. Releasing on a chord takes it immediately, even
before the delay is up.

## Why

The problem cannot be solved by arrangement, because it is not a spatial problem: the two
chords are functionally adjacent and harmonically distant at the same time. Time is the
dimension that is free.

It also gives the player a choice rather than a rule — dragging slowly takes the intermediate
chords along, which is a real musical option.

## Rejected

_Moving the chords closer together._ Would have to break either the voice-leading neighbourhood
or the tension axis.

_A direct connection drawn around the tonic._ Kept as a visual hint only; on its own it does
not stop the tonic from being triggered.
