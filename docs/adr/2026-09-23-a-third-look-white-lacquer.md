# A third look: white lacquer

Date: 2026-09-23 · Status: proposed, extends 2026-09-23-two-looks-grown-and-built

## Context

The built look answered the wish for something straight and serious, but it stayed on the same ground as the grown
one: graphite with a warm accent, colour carrying fitness. Asked what "expensive" would look like, the answer that
came back was none of that — white, straight, a cool light, nothing else.

## Decision

A third look, polished, on the geometry the built look already uses: straight bars, even radii, the map in its grid.
Only the light changes.

Everything is white lacquer. Fitness is read as brightness alone — what carries is nearly white, what pulls falls away
into slate — and the trace of blue in it is the light on the surface, not a colour of its own. The chosen chord is the
one thing that glows, pale blue. The tonic keeps its seam, now a clear blue line that survives on white, and the ring
around the tonic's chord turns white so it cannot be mistaken for the chosen one. Names sit on their shapes in dark
ink. The surroundings leave the key's hue behind and hold one cool grey, because a warm frame would undo the whole
point.

The map drops the warm-and-cool hues it uses elsewhere: where a chord leads is already said by its place — up is
home, down is away — so the hue has nothing left to add.

## Why

The two existing looks are variations of one temperament. This is a different one: the instrument as equipment, where
restraint is the statement. It costs nothing to carry, because the geometry is already there and only the palette
branches.

Brightness is also the most robust channel there is. It survives a phone in sunlight, a projector and a photograph,
and it needs no colour vision at all.

## Rejected

_A bright page as well._ White shapes on a white ground would have to carry their fitness in outlines instead of in
light, and the field would lose its depth.

_Keeping the warm and cool hues on the map._ Two channels saying the same thing, one of which would have muddied the
white.

_Replacing the built look._ Straight and graphite is what some people asked for; this adds a temperament rather than
trading one for another.
