# The map names functions, not chords

## Context

A player who learns that the second field is "G" has learned one key. A player who learns it is
the dominant has learned all of them.

## Decision

Chord fields carry the function in the large label — tonic, dominant, subdominant, parallel,
counter-chord — with the chord name small underneath. Melody stripes carry no label at all by
default; scale degrees, movable-do syllables or note names can be switched on.

## Why

The player asked for the key-specific detail to be abstracted away, and it is the right
instinct: function is what transfers between keys, and naming it is most of what harmony
teaching does.

Leaving the melody stripes unlabelled by default keeps the surface calm and forces the ear to
lead. Anyone who wants the names can have them in one click.

## Rejected

_Chord names as the primary label._ Concrete, immediately useful, and it teaches nothing that
survives a change of key.
