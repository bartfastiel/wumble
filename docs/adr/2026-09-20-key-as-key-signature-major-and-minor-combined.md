# Key = key signature; major and minor stay combined

Date: 2026-09-20 · Status: accepted (deliberate trade-off)

## Context

Beginners look for "C major" and count sharps on a sheet; they do not look for "A minor" separately. But styles that
bring their own scale (modes, techno, blues) build on a root – and with "C major / A minor" as one entry that yields
C Dorian, not A Dorian.

## Decision

One entry per key signature, named after both of its keys. In minor and modal styles the song list honestly names the
root ("A minor", "root D").

## Consequences

A known inconsistency, documented rather than hidden. Splitting the setting into root plus gender is designed, not
implemented.

## Rejected

_Root plus major/minor as two settings._ Correct, and one question more before the first note – the instrument would
start with a form instead of a sound.
